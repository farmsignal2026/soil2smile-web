About Soil2Smile

Healthy soil. Happy life.

Soil2Smile is a precision-agriculture company. We make satellite and remote-sensing data usable for people who work the land — growers, field officers, and the organizations that depend on a healthy crop.

FarmSignal is our flagship product, refined season by season with the teams using it across crop programs.
