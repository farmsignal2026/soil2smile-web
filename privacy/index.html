FarmSignal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

FarmSignal is a crop field-monitoring app for farm officers. It is operated by Soil2Smile Technologies (OPC) Pvt Ltd ("we", "us") and published on Google Play by its developer, Saravanan. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is protected.

Who the app is for

FarmSignal is a working tool for farm officers and supervisors of mills, agribusinesses and other agricultural organisations we serve. You need an account issued by your employer (a phone number and a PIN) to use it. It is not an app for the general public.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details: your phone number and PIN. The PIN is stored only as a securely hashed password by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth); we never see or store it in plain text.
  • Precise location (GPS): used only while you are using the app, to record field boundaries (GeoTag), to confirm you are at the field before a scout or follow-up report or photo is accepted, and to show your position on the map. The app does not track your location in the background.
  • Photos: pictures you take of fields and crops for scouting, follow-up and boundary records. Each photo is stored with its location and time.
  • Profile information: your name, role and the mill, division or area you are assigned to, supplied by your employer.
  • Crop and field records: scout checklists, severity grades, recommendations, remarks and follow-up answers that you enter, linked to the fields and the farmers who own them (farmer name, phone number and plot details).

Information stored on your phone

To keep the app usable when mobile signal is weak, FarmSignal saves a copy of the most recent field, scout and checklist data in the app's private storage on your phone. This copy is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to show each officer the fields they are responsible for, to record and review field visits, to calculate crop-health status from satellite imagery, and to produce reports for your employer. We do not use it for advertising.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Who processes the data

Data is stored and processed on our behalf by our infrastructure provider, Supabase (database, file storage and authentication), and satellite crop-health data is processed with Google Earth Engine. These providers handle data only to operate FarmSignal for us. Your employer can see the records created by officers in its own area.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access is restricted by role: an officer can see only the fields in the mill and division assigned to them.

Retention and deletion

We keep records for as long as your employer uses FarmSignal, because crop records are kept for agricultural record-keeping. To ask for your personal account information to be corrected or deleted, contact us at the address below, or ask your employer's administrator.

Children

FarmSignal is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
